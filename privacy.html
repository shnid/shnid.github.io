Privacy policy

Chief of staff health is a personal tool used only by its owner. Last updated October 17, 2026.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Google Health data (for example sleep, activity, heart rate and weight) through the Google Health API. Access is read-only: it never changes, deletes or writes anything in Google.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner summaries and suggestions about their own health and fitness. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy.

Where it is stored

Data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. To produce summaries, the owner's AI assistant processes the data on the owner's behalf.

Other users

The tool has no users other than its owner and collects no data about anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the local data deleted from the owner's computer.
